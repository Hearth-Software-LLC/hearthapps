Questions

Which devices does Suffice work on?
iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, iPhone Duo, and Apple Watch, plus widgets.
How does my partner join?
Invite them from Household. Parents and adults share the budget with you.
Can I get my data out?
Yes. Export to CSV whenever you like. Your own data is never behind a paywall.
