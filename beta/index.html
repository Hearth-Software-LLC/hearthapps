Public betas

Try the betas.

Suffice, Sated and Hearken are in public beta through TestFlight, Apple’s free app for trying apps before they reach the App Store. Joining takes about a minute.

How to join

  1. 1

    Get TestFlight

    On your iPhone or iPad, install TestFlight from the App Store. It’s free and made by Apple.

    Get TestFlight
  2. 2

    Open an invitation

    Still on your iPhone or iPad, tap Join the beta for any app below. It opens straight in TestFlight. On a computer? Send this page to your phone first.

  3. 3

    Accept and install

    Tap Accept, then Install. The app lands on your Home Screen with an orange dot next to its name, which marks it as a beta.

Good to know

What you need

An iPhone or iPad on iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 and an Apple Account. Features that use your iPhone’s built-in AI need iPhone 15 Pro or newer.

Updates arrive in TestFlight

New builds show up in TestFlight, and you can turn on automatic updates there. Each build works for 90 days, and a newer one usually arrives well before then.

Sending feedback

Take a screenshot while using a beta and TestFlight offers to send it with a note. You can also write to swarren@mail.com — every message is read.

Leaving a beta

Open TestFlight, choose the app and tap Stop Testing, or simply delete the app. When an app launches, you can install it from the App Store in place of the beta.

Invitation not working?

Make sure TestFlight is installed and you’re opening the link on the iPhone or iPad you want to test on. Betas can fill up; if one is full, write to us and we’ll let you know when there’s room.
