BigTable

Meal planning & groceries

Feed the whole crew.
Without the 5 p.m. panic.

Plan breakfast, lunch and dinner for the week around everyone’s likes, dislikes and allergies, then turn the plan into a grocery list sorted by aisle. The whole household plans together.

  • Plan My WeekDescribe the week you want and get a full plan to preview before it lands.
  • Lists by aisleGenerate groceries for any date range, check items off as you shop, and share the list.
  • Recipes in, any waySay it, type it, scan a cookbook page or paste a link. Servings scale for you.
  • The household, togetherInvite family, set who can edit the plan, and follow cooks in the community.

Suffice

Household budget

Money talk,
minus the tension.

See every account’s cash at a glance, every transaction with who made it, budgets with subcategories, and the bills coming due — shared between partners through your own iCloud. No accounts to create. No servers holding your ledger.

  • Budgets as ringsPlan a month, split it into subcategories, and watch what’s left at a glance.
  • Bills that remindDue this week, subscriptions, and overdue — mark paid and it files itself.
  • Ask Suffice“How much did we spend eating out?” Answered on your iPhone, by voice or text.
  • Locked with Face IDAmounts hide on the Lock Screen. Export to CSV whenever you like.

Sated

Calories & macros

Know what you ate.
Not just guess.

Track calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber with a 13,000-food library that works offline, a barcode scanner, and a nutrition-label reader that checks its own math. A coach that lives on your iPhone answers with your real numbers.

  • Say It“Two eggs and toast.” Sated splits it into foods and amounts, and you confirm.
  • Scan labels & barcodesThe label reader runs on the device and flags any numbers that don’t add up.
  • A private coachAsk what to eat for dinner and log the suggestion with one tap. Nothing leaves your phone.
  • Day, week, monthSummaries written from your exact log, plus widgets and Apple Health.

Hearken

Scripture study

Read with purpose.
Remember what you learn.

Seven subjects with mastery meters, short games that use spaced repetition, a library of readings, and a reader made for highlighting and notes. Your study stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

  • A reader for marking upTap a verse to highlight in your own color legend, underline, or add a note inline.
  • Mastery you can seeRings and tiers for every subject and unit, earned through recall — not just reading.
  • Games that teachDaily Challenge, Chronology, Unit Checks and Spaced Review, with streaks and XP.
  • No ads. No tracking.Sign in with Apple is optional. Every feature works without it.

Trove

Important documents & where they live

Every important paper.
Found in seconds.

Passports, licenses, birth certificates, insurance cards and warranties — scanned once, tied to the person or thing they belong to, and to the drawer or safe where the original is kept. Encrypted in your own iCloud and shared with your household.

  • Scan it, and it reads itselfThe camera reads a license’s barcode and a passport’s lines, and on-device intelligence fills in the rest for you to check.
  • Tied to people and thingsEach document belongs to someone in your household, a car or the house, so the right one is always a tap away.
  • Know where the original isNote the fire safe, the file box or the glovebox — with a yearly reminder to check each place.
  • Renewals before they’re urgentReminders start with enough lead time to renew a passport or license, then a week before, then on the day.

Ember

The voice of the Hearth apps

Just ask.
Your apps answer.

Ask what’s for dinner, what’s left in the grocery budget, or what tonight’s reading is. Ember answers out loud from your own Hearth apps, on iPhone and Apple Watch — and it thinks on your device first.

  • Voice firstTap and talk, or ask through Siri and the Action button. Answers are spoken a sentence at a time, and you can cut in.
  • Knows your Hearth appsThis week’s meals from BigTable, the grocery budget in Suffice, allowances in Caloma and today’s reading in Hearken.
  • Private by designIt answers on your iPhone when it can. Cloud help is your choice, and who you are is stripped out before anything leaves.
  • On your wristAsk from Apple Watch, even when your iPhone isn’t nearby, over Wi-Fi or cellular.

Built the same way,
on purpose.

The hard things in life are personal. So every Hearth app follows the same rules.

Your data stays yours

Suffice, Sated, Caloma, Hearken and Trove keep your information on your devices and in your private iCloud. No ads, no tracking, nothing sold.

Intelligence on the device

Coaches, voice logging and money questions run on your iPhone’s built-in AI, so your words and numbers never leave it. Ember asks before it uses the cloud, and strips out who you are first.

Native to the core

Written in SwiftUI with Liquid Glass, widgets and Apple Watch apps. They feel like they came with your iPhone.

Made for households

Partners share a budget, parents share the chore board, families plan meals together. Hard things are easier shared.
