Questions

Which devices does Trove work on?
iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, and iPhone Duo.
Who can see my documents?
You, and the people in your household you share them with. They’re encrypted in your own iCloud.
Does Trove replace the originals?
No. It keeps a scan and tells you where the original is, so you can find it when you need it.
