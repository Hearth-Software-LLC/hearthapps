Plan

The week, decided before Monday.

Tell Plan My Week what kind of week it is — busy, cheap, more fish — and preview a full plan before anything lands on the calendar.

  • Breakfast, lunch and dinner on one screen
  • Swap any meal, or drag a recipe onto a night

Recipes

Every recipe. Any way in.

Say it, type it, scan a cookbook page or paste a link. BigTable cleans it up and scales the servings for however many are at the table.

  • A handwritten card and a food blog land in the same tidy format
  • Servings scale every ingredient for you
  • Tag, search and favorite what your family loves

Groceries for any range of days, sorted by aisle. Checked items sink, and everyone shopping sees the same list.

Questions

Which devices does BigTable work on?
iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, iPhone Duo, and Apple Watch.
How does my family join?
Invite them from Household. Parents and adults share the kitchen with you, and the adults you invite share your BigTable Plus or Hearth+.
What happens to my recipes if I cancel Plus?
Nothing. Your recipes, plans and lists stay yours and keep working. Only the Plus features stop.
Where can I get help?
Visit Support or write to support@hearthapps.com. A real person reads every message.
