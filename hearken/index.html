Reader

Tap a verse to highlight in your own color legend, underline, or add a note inline. Ask for an explanation when a passage is hard.

Every subject and unit has a ring and tiers, earned through recall — not just reading.

Questions

Which devices does Hearken work on?
iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, iPhone Duo, and Apple Watch.
Do I need an account?
No. Sign in with Apple is optional, and every feature works without it.

No. Hearken is an independent study app. It is not made, sponsored or endorsed by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.
