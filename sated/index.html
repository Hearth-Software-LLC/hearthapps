Questions

Which devices does Sated work on?
iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, iPhone Duo, and Apple Watch, plus Apple Health.
Does it need a signal?
No. The food library works offline, and the coach and label reader run on your iPhone.
Is Sated a medical app?
No. It helps you track what you eat. Talk to your doctor or a dietitian about medical or dietary needs.
