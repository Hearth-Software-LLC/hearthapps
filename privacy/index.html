Who we are

Hearth apps — BigTable, Suffice, Sated, Caloma and Hearken — are made by Hearth Software LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information each app handles, where it is kept, who else is involved, and the choices you have. Where an app’s description below differs from this general summary, the app’s description controls.

Across the apps

Your Hearth account

Signing in uses Sign in with Apple. It creates one Hearth account, kept by our sign-in provider (Supabase), that BigTable, Suffice, Sated and Hearken share on your device, so signing in or out in one does the same in the others. The account holds the identifier Apple gives us, your name and the email address Apple shares (which may be a private relay address). Caloma doesn’t sign in to the account; it keeps your Apple sign-in on your device and offers it to Hearken, and it uses your Hearth account ID for purchases if another Hearth app on the device is signed in.

Suffice and Sated also create an anonymous session, with no name or email, so optional online features (such as restaurant menus) can tell our server a request came from the app. Nothing from your budget or food log is stored with it.

Your Hearth household

Every Hearth app works inside one household: the people in it, their roles, colors and photos, and, if you add them, birthdays, allergies, diets, likes, dislikes and household food rules. The household is kept in the iCloud account of the person who set it up and shared with the people they invite through iCloud sharing. Birthdays, allergies, diets, likes, dislikes and food rules are stored with iCloud’s end-to-end encrypted fields. Names, roles, colors and photos are protected by iCloud’s standard encryption. We never receive the Hearth household itself; BigTable keeps who is in your BigTable household on our servers (see below).

Parents can change anyone in the household, adults can change their own details, and kids can view it. Each app shares its own data with the household: parents and adults share BigTable’s kitchen and Suffice’s budget, and everyone, kids included, shares and uses Caloma.

Sharing between apps on your device

Hearth apps on the same iPhone or iPad pass a few things to each other through a private area of your device that only our apps can read; none of it goes through our servers. BigTable shares the meal plan with Caloma and Sated; the apps share your family’s names and photos; Suffice shares the grocery budget and grocery receipts with BigTable; Caloma shares allowance payouts with Suffice; Hearken shares your reading plan with Caloma.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are made through Apple. We use RevenueCat to check what you’ve bought; it receives a purchase identifier (your Hearth account ID when you’re signed in, otherwise a random ID) and your purchase history from Apple. We never see your payment details. If you are in someone’s household and sign in to BigTable, our server checks the household organizer’s plan so it can unlock BigTable, Suffice, Sated and Hearken for you.

App by app

BigTable

Your recipes and their photos, tags, meal plans, Dine Out picks, grocery lists and pantry are kept on your device and in your household’s iCloud. BigTable keeps the following on servers we operate, so a household can plan together and Community works:

  • Account: your Hearth account, profile name and photo, notification settings and, if you allow notifications, a device token.
  • Household membership: who is in your BigTable household, their names and photos, what each person may change, and invitations. Older versions of BigTable kept more family details on our servers; we remove them as households move to iCloud.
  • Community: recipes you publish and their photos, your public profile, likes, favorites of published recipes, follows, blocks and any reports you send. If your profile is visible, other BigTable users can see your display name, photo and published recipes. You can hide your profile in Profile › Privacy, which also unpublishes your recipes.
  • Plans and usage: your plan and how many AI requests you’ve made this month, so we can apply limits.

Recipes and plans that were on our servers before BigTable moved to iCloud are copied into your iCloud once. We delete our copy of your plans, grocery lists, tags and household details 30 days later; recipes you published, or that others saved, stay.

Some features use AI on our servers (see Intelligence). Dine Out searches for restaurants near you with Apple Maps on your device; your location isn’t sent to us. Snack searches go from your device to Open Food Facts, restaurant logos come from Logo.dev, and a recipe link you import is fetched from that website.

Suffice

Your accounts, balances, transactions, budgets, bills, stashes, loans, receipts and statements are stored on your device and synced through iCloud. Their details are end-to-end encrypted; receipt images, statements and photos use iCloud’s standard encryption. Parents and adults in your household see the same budget, including transactions from banks each of them connects. We do not keep your ledger.

If you connect a bank, you sign in to your bank with Plaid, not with us. Our server keeps the bank’s name and Plaid’s access token for the connection, sealed with encryption, and passes your accounts and transactions from Plaid to the app without storing them. Disconnecting the bank, or erasing Suffice’s data, deletes the token. Plaid’s handling of your information is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy. Merchant logos come from Logo.dev, using the merchant’s web address.

The camera is used to photograph receipts, and the microphone and speech recognition to talk to Ask Suffice, which runs on your iPhone. Face ID is handled by iOS; we never receive biometric data.

Sated

Your food log, goals and settings are stored on your device and in your iCloud. Food search uses a database built into the app, and nutrition labels are read with your camera on the device; photos are not uploaded.

  • Barcodes: the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product.
  • Restaurants: to find places near you, your location is used with Apple Maps on your device and isn’t saved or sent to us. To show menus, the names of nearby restaurants (and a restaurant’s web address, for its logo) are sent to our server, which looks them up with FatSecret and Logo.dev. A meal you log from a restaurant saves the restaurant’s name in your food log in iCloud.
  • Apple Health: with your permission, Sated writes the calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat and fiber you log to Apple Health and reads your active energy. Health data is never used for advertising and is never sent to our servers.

Caloma

Family members, photos, chores and chore photos, rewards, points and allowance, calendar events, lists, whiteboard notes, rooms, home-care tasks and Home settings are stored on your device and synced through iCloud with everyone in your household, kids included. We do not operate a server that receives your family’s information.

  • Apple Home: Home Control shows and controls your own HomeKit accessories through Apple. Talk uses the microphone to speak through a doorbell or camera; nothing is recorded.
  • Family Board faces: if you turn on face recognition on a wall iPad, Caloma stores a numeric description of each person’s face (not a photo) on that iPad only. It is never synced, backed up or sent anywhere, and you can forget a face or turn recognition off at any time. The camera may also check for someone nearby and the room’s brightness, without saving images.
  • Calendars and photos: if you allow it, Caloma shows your other Apple calendars and can add family events to them. Screensaver photos you pick are copied onto that iPad only.

Hearken

Highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading plans and progress, study activity, mastery, settings and imported books are stored on your device and in your iCloud. The original files of imported books stay on your device.

Study groups are kept on our servers so members can study together. When you join one, we store your display name and initials, your role, your reading progress in the group, and what you share there: highlights, reflections, questions, answers, comments and reactions, plus your group notification settings and a device token for notifications. Everyone in a group can see what is shared in it; anyone with its invite code can see its name, plan, owner’s name and number of members. Photos are not shared with groups. People you block, and reports you send, are kept on our servers so we can hide their content from you and act on reports.

Some optional features connect to other providers: the listening voice is downloaded from Hugging Face (or GitHub), embedded videos play from YouTube, and links open external websites. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address.

Intelligence

Most of the intelligence in Hearth apps runs on your device using Apple’s built-in models: Sated’s coach and Say It, Ask Suffice, Hearken’s Explain, Caloma’s list icons and handwriting, and BigTable’s first attempt at link imports, scans, meal plans and grocery lists. That data isn’t sent to us or to an AI provider. Speech is transcribed on the device where it can be, and otherwise by Apple’s speech recognition.

Some BigTable features use AI on our servers: creating or changing a recipe with AI, and meal plans, grocery lists, scans and imports when the device can’t do them. The request carries what the feature needs: the recipe, plan or page text, photos you scan, and your household’s ages, roles, allergies, diets, likes and dislikes, without names or photos. We send it to Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (transcribing recipe recordings) or Google (Gemini, for generated recipe photos), which process it to return a result. We don’t store the request, apart from counting it toward your monthly limit.

Children

Caloma, and the Hearth household, are designed for parents to use with their children. Information about children, such as names, photos, birthdays, allergies and chore history, is entered by a parent and kept in the family’s iCloud; we do not receive it. Children in a household may use Caloma on their own devices, where they see the family’s Caloma information. BigTable households may include children’s names and photos as household members on our servers.

Hearken study groups and BigTable Community are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting data

Data in your iCloud stays for as long as you keep it, and data on our servers stays while your account or group is active. Here is how to delete it:

AppIn the appWhat it removes
BigTableProfile › Delete AccountYour Hearth account (see below). Recipes you created stay without your name. If you organize a household, remove its members first.
SufficeProfile › Erase App Data, or Profile › Delete AccountErase removes your budget on the device and in iCloud, and your bank connections. Delete Account also deletes your Hearth account.
SatedSettings › Your Data › Erase Sated Data, or Settings › Account › Delete AccountErase removes your food log on the device and in iCloud, what Sated wrote to Apple Health, and your goals and settings. Delete Account also deletes your Hearth account.
CalomaFamily › Data › Erase All Caloma DataCaloma’s data on the device and, for the household owner, in iCloud, plus saved faces.
HearkenSettings › Account › Delete AccountYour Hearth account, everything you wrote in study groups, and your Hearken data on the device and in iCloud. Groups you own pass to another member.

Delete Account in BigTable, Suffice, Sated or Hearken deletes your Hearth account for every Hearth app, with everything our servers keep for you: BigTable’s server data, your study-group posts, bank connections, blocks, reports and your purchase record at RevenueCat. Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so cancel them in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. The household is separate: remove people, or leave the household, from Household in any app. For anything not listed here, email us and we will delete it. Posts and comments deleted from study groups are permanently removed after 30 days. We keep records of purchases as long as we need them for accounting and legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can use Suffice, Sated, Caloma and Hearken without signing in. You can turn iCloud off for an app in Settings › [your name] › iCloud, and change permissions for the camera, microphone, photos, location, calendars, Home, notifications and Health at any time in iOS Settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Email us to make a request; we will respond within the time required by law. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

Security

We rely on Apple’s platform protections, including iCloud encryption and the Keychain. Information sent to our servers travels over encrypted connections and is protected by access controls; bank access tokens are additionally encrypted. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

This website

This website does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may log basic technical information, such as IP addresses and pages requested, to keep the site running and secure.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the apps change. We’ll post the new version here with a new date, and for significant changes we’ll let you know in the app. Questions or requests? Email info@hearthapps.com.
