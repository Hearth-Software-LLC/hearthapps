Chores

Chores kids actually want to do.

Each kid gets a ring that fills as chores get done, a celebration when it closes, and a photo when you ask for one.

  • Photo proof when you ask for it
  • Approve what needs your OK in one place

Questions

Which devices does Caloma work on?
iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, iPhone Duo, and Apple Watch.
Do my kids need their own iPhone?
No. Kid View works on a shared iPad or any device in the household.
How do both parents share it?
Invite each other from Household. The whole family shares the same chores, rewards and calendar.
Where can I get help?
Visit Support or write to support@hearthapps.com. A real person reads every message.
